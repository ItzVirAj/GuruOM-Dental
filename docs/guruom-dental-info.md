## 4. Advanced Technology & In-House Dental Lab Infrastructure

GuruOm Dental integrates hospital-grade surgical technologies with an in-house digital dental lab:

### A. Digital Diagnostics & 3D Imaging
- **High-Resolution 3D CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography):** Provides 0.075 mm sub-millimeter volumetric 3D reconstruction of maxillary and mandibular bone structures, locating mandibular canals, maxillary sinuses, and bone densities with ultra-low radiation dose protocols.
- **Digital RVG Intraoral Sensors:** Replaces chemical film X-rays with instantaneous chairside digital radiographs reducing radiation exposure by up to 90%.
- **Intraoral 3D Optical Scanner:** Digital wand that captures 3,000 frames per second, replacing uncomfortable impression trays and eliminating patient gag reflexes.

### B. Micro-Surgical & Operatory Technology
- **Carl Zeiss Dental Operating Microscopes:** Provides up to 25x optical magnification and coaxial shadow-free xenon illumination to navigate calcified micro-canals and hidden accessory anatomy.
- **Computer-Controlled Anesthetic Delivery System:** Automated fluid dynamics deliver painless, pressure-controlled subcutaneous local anesthesia.
- **Multi-Wavelength Soft-Tissue Diode Lasers:** Enables bloodless, scalpel-free gum recontouring, frenectomy, and deep pocket bacterial decontamination with accelerated tissue regeneration.

### C. Digital In-House Dental Lab (CAD/CAM)
- **5-Axis Milling Machine:** Mills monolithic zirconia, PMMA, and lithium disilicate (E.max) blocks directly from intraoral digital scans with 10-micron precision.
- **Automated Sintering Furnaces:** High-temperature sintering furnaces achieve high fracture resistance (1,200+ MPa) and lifelike optical translucency for ceramic crowns and bridges.
- **3D Dental Printing:** Precision biocompatible 3D printing for surgical implant guides, orthodontic models, clear aligner staging trays, and occlusal night guards.

## 5. Scope of Clinical Care & Procedure Directory

### 1. General & Preventive Dentistry
- **Digital Prophylaxis & Ultrasonic Scaling:** Gentle, computer-tuned piezo scaling removes calculus and bacterial biofilm without scraping protective enamel.
- **Air Polishing:** Subgingival glycine powder air-polishing removes tea, coffee, and tobacco stains safely.
- **Tooth-Colored Biomimetic Composite Fillings:** Nanohybrid composite layered with natural optical translucency.

### 2. Dental Implants & Tooth Replacement
- **Single-Tooth Implants:** Swiss & German grade-IV titanium or zirconia fixtures placed with guided flapless surgery.
- **Full-Arch Immediate Loading (All-on-4 / All-on-6):** Fixed permanent teeth anchored to four or six angled implants.
- **Bone Regeneration & Sinus Lifts:** Bio-Oss and PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin) biological growth factor grafting for bone enhancement.

### 3. Root Canal Treatment & Tooth Preservation
- **Single-Sitting Microscopic RCT:** Complete canal cleaning, shaping with flexible nickel-titanium files, and warm 3D gutta-percha hermetic obturation in 45 minutes.
- **Crown Restorations:** Monolithic Zirconia and E.max pressed ceramic crowns tailored to patient bite dynamics.

### 4. Cosmetic Dentistry & Smile Design
- **Handcrafted Ceramic Veneers:** 0.3 mm micro-invasive porcelain laminates crafted to correct spacing, chips, and discolorations.
- **In-Clinic Laser Teeth Whitening:** Safe chemical and laser activation lifting shades by 4–8 tones in a single session.
- **Direct Composite Smile Bonding:** Chairside aesthetic reshaping for quick single-visit improvements.

### 5. Orthodontics & Clear Aligners
- **Custom Invisible Clear Aligners:** Sequenced series of BPA-free medical polyurethane trays designed via 3D computer motion planning.
- **Self-Ligating Low-Friction Braces:** Reduced friction brackets for faster, more comfortable tooth alignment.

### 6. Pediatric Dental Care
- **Fear-Free Operatory Environment:** Specialized behavioral guidance and non-threatening dental equipment.
- **Protective Enamel Treatments:** High-viscosity glass ionomer pit and fissure sealants plus topical fluoride varnish.

### 7. Emergency Dental Care & Trauma Triage
- Immediate care for avulsed teeth, fractured crowns, displaced restorations, acute pulpitis, and facial dental swelling.
